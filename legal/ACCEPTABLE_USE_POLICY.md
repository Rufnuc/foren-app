# Acceptable Use Policy

**Effective date:** 23 September 2026

This Acceptable Use Policy ("AUP") forms part of the [Terms of Service](TERMS_OF_SERVICE.md). It describes what you may and may not do with Foren. By using Foren you agree to this AUP.

Foren exists to help people safely document and verify incidents that have happened to them. Use it for that purpose, lawfully and in good faith.

## 1. Permitted use

You may use Foren to:

- Document, organise, and analyse records of incidents you have experienced or witnessed (e.g. harassment, stalking, abuse).
- Capture content you are lawfully entitled to record or possess (e.g. messages sent to you, screenshots of communications you received).
- Create tamper-evidence commitments and export records for your own use or to share with people you choose (e.g. police, a solicitor, a school, an employer, a support service).

## 2. Prohibited use

You must **not** use Foren to:

1. **Fabricate, falsify, alter, or backdate** records, or otherwise create misleading evidence. Foren's integrity features are designed to detect tampering; misuse undermines real survivors and may be a criminal offence (e.g. perverting the course of justice / perjury).
2. **Harass, stalk, surveil, intimidate, threaten, dox, or harm** any person, or to facilitate any of these.
3. **Unlawfully record, intercept, or collect** communications or data — for example, recording private conversations or accessing devices/accounts without the legal right to do so.
4. Store or distribute **child sexual abuse material (CSAM)** or any content whose mere possession is illegal. (If you have encountered CSAM, report it to the appropriate authority such as the police or the [Internet Watch Foundation](https://www.iwf.org.uk); do not store it in Foren.)
5. Infringe others' **intellectual-property** or **privacy** rights beyond what is lawful for documenting your own incident.
6. Use the Service to **defame** or make knowingly false allegations against others.
7. Attempt to **breach security**, reverse-engineer (except where permitted by law), disrupt, overload, or circumvent rate limits or protections of the Service or its networks.
8. Use the Service to **violate any applicable law** or the rights of others.

## 3. Honest documentation

Foren records are most useful when they are accurate. Do not edit machine-generated text to misrepresent what was said. Where you correct transcriptions or classifications, do so truthfully. The credibility of your record depends on its honesty.

## 4. Reporting misuse

If you become aware of misuse of the Service, contact israel.egede@outlook.com. Because of Foren's zero-knowledge design, we generally cannot see user content and therefore cannot police it; responsibility for lawful use rests with users.

## 5. Consequences

Violating this AUP may result in suspension or termination of access (where technically possible) and may expose you to civil or criminal liability. Nothing in this AUP requires us to monitor content, and we are not able to in the ordinary course given the encryption model.

## 6. Safety note

Foren is a documentation tool, not protection from immediate harm. **If you are in danger, contact emergency services.** See the [Disclaimer](DISCLAIMER.md) and the in-app Help & Safety section for crisis resources.
