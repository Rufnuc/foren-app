# Cookie & Local Storage Policy

**Effective date:** 23 September 2026

This policy explains how Foren uses cookies and similar on-device storage technologies. It supplements the [Privacy Policy](PRIVACY_POLICY.md).

## 1. Summary

Foren is privacy-first and **does not use advertising or third-party tracking cookies**. We do not profile you, and we do not share data with advertisers. The storage Foren uses is **strictly necessary** to make the app work on your device.

## 2. What we use and why

| Technology | Purpose | Type | Stored where |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Local storage** | Hold your vault configuration (e.g. salted passcode hash, chosen disguise, settings flags) | Strictly necessary | Your device only |
| **IndexedDB** (on-device database) | Store your encrypted cases, evidence, attachments, and commitments | Strictly necessary | Your device only |
| **Session memory** | Hold your decryption key only while the vault is unlocked | Strictly necessary | Your device memory (cleared on lock) |
| **Cache storage / service worker** *(if used for offline)* | Make the app work offline and load quickly | Strictly necessary | Your device only |

This information stays on your device and is not transmitted to us.

## 3. What we do **not** use

- No advertising cookies.
- No third-party analytics or tracking cookies inside the vault.
- No cross-site tracking or fingerprinting for marketing.
- No social-media tracking pixels.

## 4. Third-party requests

Some optional features cause your browser to contact third parties (for example, downloading the on-device transcription model, or anchoring a hash via a public network). These are described in the [Privacy Policy](PRIVACY_POLICY.md). They are not cookies, but they may be visible to those third parties as ordinary network requests (e.g. your IP address). They occur only when you use the relevant feature.

## 5. Consent

Because the storage above is **strictly necessary** to provide a service you have requested, it does not require consent under UK/EU rules (PECR / ePrivacy). If we ever introduce any non-essential cookies, we will ask for your consent first and update this policy.

## 6. Managing storage

You control this data on your device:

- Deleting cases/evidence in-app, or using **panic-wipe**, removes stored data.
- Clearing your browser's site data, or uninstalling the app, removes Foren's local storage.
- Note: clearing storage will remove your vault and any evidence not backed up or exported. Make sure you have your recovery method first.

## 7. Changes

We may update this policy; the "Effective date" reflects the latest version.
