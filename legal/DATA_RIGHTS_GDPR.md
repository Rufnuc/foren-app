# Your Data Rights (UK GDPR / EU GDPR)

**Effective date:** 23 September 2026

> *This summary supplements, and is governed by, the full [Privacy Policy](PRIVACY_POLICY.md).*

Foren is **local-first and zero-knowledge**: your evidence is encrypted and stored on your device, and we are designed not to be able to read it. This changes how data-protection rights work in practice — for most of your data, **you exercise these rights directly in the app**, because you hold the data and the keys.

## How local-first changes your rights

| Right (UK/EU GDPR) | What it means | How it works in Foren |
|---|---|---|
| **Be informed** (Arts. 13–14) | Know what is processed and why | See the [Privacy Policy](PRIVACY_POLICY.md) |
| **Access** (Art. 15) | Get a copy of your data | Your data is on your device; **Export** produces a full copy. For the little we hold, email israel.egede@outlook.com |
| **Rectification** (Art. 16) | Correct inaccurate data | Edit cases, evidence, and machine-generated text directly in the app |
| **Erasure** (Art. 17) | Delete your data | Delete cases/evidence in-app; permanent after the soft-delete window; **panic-wipe** erases everything. *Note: hashes already anchored to a blockchain are public and cannot be deleted — see below* |
| **Restriction** (Art. 18) | Limit processing | Stop using features; data stays inert on your device |
| **Portability** (Art. 20) | Take your data elsewhere | **Export** gives you a structured, machine-readable bundle |
| **Object** (Art. 21) | Object to processing | Decline optional features; contact israel.egede@outlook.com for any server-side processing |
| **Rights re: automated decisions** (Art. 22) | Not be subject to solely automated decisions with legal/significant effects | Foren's AI is **assistive only**; you confirm or override every output. No solely-automated decisions are made about you |
| **Withdraw consent** (Art. 7(3)) | Turn off consented processing | Stop using the optional feature at any time |
| **Complain** | Raise concerns with a regulator | Contact your local data protection authority |

## The blockchain exception to erasure

When you choose to **anchor** a record, a one-way cryptographic **hash** (not your evidence) is published to a public, immutable ledger. Such hashes:

- reveal nothing about you or your content and cannot be reversed;
- **cannot be deleted** by us or anyone, because the ledger is immutable and decentralised.

You can always delete the underlying evidence on your device. Please consider this permanence before anchoring.

## Special-category and criminal-offence data

Evidence of abuse may include **special-category data** (Art. 9) and **criminal-offence data** (Art. 10). Because this data stays under your control on your device, we do not determine the purposes or means of processing it. Where you choose a feature that sends such data through us (e.g. emergency dispatch), we rely on your **explicit consent** and applicable conditions under the Data Protection Act 2018.

## Making a request

For data we actually hold, email **israel.egede@outlook.com**. We will respond within **one month** (extendable by two months for complex requests, with notice). We may ask you to verify your identity — though we often cannot identify you, because we hold so little about you. There is normally no fee.

## Supervisory authority

You can complain to **your local data protection authority** (for the UK, the Information Commissioner's Office, https://ico.org.uk). We'd appreciate the chance to address your concern first: israel.egede@outlook.com.
