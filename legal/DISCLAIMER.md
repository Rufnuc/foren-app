# Disclaimer

**Effective date:** 23 September 2026

Please read this carefully. It explains important limitations of Foren.

## 1. Not an emergency service

**Foren cannot help you in an emergency.** If you are in immediate danger or fear for your safety, contact your local emergency number now:

- **UK:** 999 (or 112). For a silent call when you cannot speak, call 999 then press 55.
- Other countries: your local emergency number (e.g. 112 in the EU, 911 in the US).

Foren does not contact emergency services for you and does not monitor your situation.

## 2. Crisis support

If you need support, consider contacting (UK examples — replace/localise as appropriate):

- **National Domestic Abuse Helpline (Refuge):** 0808 2000 247 (24/7)
- **National Stalking Helpline:** 0808 802 0300
- **Samaritans:** 116 123
- **Silent Solution (police, when you can't speak):** 999 then 55

## 3. Not legal advice

Foren and its documentation do not constitute legal advice and do not create a lawyer–client relationship. Laws on harassment, stalking, abuse, evidence, recording, and data protection vary by jurisdiction and change over time. **Consult a qualified solicitor/attorney** about your specific situation, including before relying on any record for legal proceedings.

## 4. No guarantee of admissibility or outcome

Foren helps you create organised, tamper-evidence records. However:

- We **do not guarantee** that any record, report, or export will be admitted as evidence, or given any particular weight, by any court, tribunal, regulator, employer, school, or authority.
- Admissibility and evidential weight are determined by the relevant decision-maker under the applicable rules of evidence, considering many factors beyond integrity (e.g. relevance, authenticity of authorship, lawful collection, hearsay rules).
- Foren's verification confirms that records were **not altered after being committed** and **existed at the time they were anchored**. It does **not** establish who created the underlying content, whether it is true, or how it was obtained.

## 5. AI and automated analysis limitations

- Foren's classification, severity scoring, and pattern tagging are **automated and may be inaccurate, incomplete, or biased**.
- On-device text extraction (OCR) and transcription (ASR) are **machine-generated and may contain errors**. Always review and correct machine-generated text before relying on it.
- AI output is **assistive only**. It is not a professional risk assessment, a diagnosis, or a determination of any fact. Do not rely on it as a substitute for professional judgement.

## 6. Security and device limitations

- The security of your evidence depends on the **security of your device** and the **secrecy of your passcode**. If your device is compromised, or someone learns your passcode, your evidence may be exposed.
- We **cannot recover** your passcode or encryption key. If you lose them (and your recovery method), your data may be permanently lost.
- Safety features (disguised entry, duress code, safety exit, panic-wipe) reduce but **do not eliminate** risk, especially against a determined adversary with control of your device.

## 7. Timestamps and accuracy

Timestamps recorded in Foren reflect your device's clock or values you enter; they are **"claimed" times** and may not be accurate. Blockchain anchoring provides an independent "no later than" time for when a record existed, not when an underlying event occurred.
