# Foren — Legal Documents

This folder contains the legal and policy documents for the Foren application. They are written to reflect Foren's **local-first, zero-knowledge** architecture: evidence is encrypted and stored on the user's device, and our servers are designed never to receive plaintext evidence.

## ⚠️ Important — read before use

> **These documents are detailed templates, not legal advice.** Foren handles exceptionally sensitive information about harassment, stalking, and abuse. Before publishing or relying on any of these documents, have them reviewed and finalised by a **qualified solicitor/attorney** in each jurisdiction where you operate. Laws on data protection, consumer rights, evidence, lawful interception, and safeguarding vary by country and change over time.
